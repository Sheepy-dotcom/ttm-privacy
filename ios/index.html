WHO RUNS THIS SITE

This game is published by Sheepydotcom. Questions: sheepydotcom@gmail.com.

WHAT THE GAME ITSELF STORES

The game keeps your high scores and the name you enter on your own device. Clearing the app's data removes them. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

The global leaderboard. When you save a score, the game also sends that one score to a leaderboard database (hosted by Supabase) so it can be shown to other players. What is sent is exactly: the name you typed, the score, the altitude reached, whether you landed, and which board it belongs to. Nothing else — no device identifier, no email, no location, no contacts. The name is limited to 9 characters, so please do not enter your real name if you would rather not be identified. Entries are public by design; a leaderboard is a published list. If you want an entry removed, contact us at the address below.

ADVERTISING

This game shows a banner ad at the top of the screen and occasional full-screen ads between runs, both supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google may read your device's advertising identifier. That identifier is shared with Google for advertising purposes; it is not shared with anyone else, and we never see it ourselves.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visit to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, and opt out of other vendors at aboutads.info/choices.

You can control ad personalisation on your own device at any time: on Android under Settings → Google → Ads, and on iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Resetting or deleting your advertising ID there breaks the link between the ads you are shown and this app.

ANALYTICS

None. This game uses no analytics service, no crash reporting and no tracking SDK of any kind. Nothing about how you play is recorded anywhere except a score you choose to save to the leaderboard.

CHILDREN

This game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has entered personal information as a leaderboard name, contact us and we will remove it.

YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you, and to object to certain processing. Contact sheepydotcom@gmail.com to make a request, and please say which leaderboard name you used so we can find the entry.

CHANGES

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
