Support

Bitcoin to the Moon · published by Sheepydotcom

Need help, found a bug, or want a leaderboard entry removed? Email sheepydotcom@gmail.com. We read every message and normally reply within a few days.

Please include your phone or tablet model and roughly what you were doing when it went wrong — it makes bugs far quicker to find.

HOW TO PLAY

Steer by dragging anywhere on the screen. Boost by holding the pad in the bottom-right, which lifts you over trouble but burns hype fuel. Three hits ends the run.

Flying close to hazards without touching them builds a combo multiplier that decays after a few seconds. Playing it safe scores badly on purpose.

MY SCORES DISAPPEARED

Scores are stored on your own device, not in an account. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them permanently and we cannot restore them. There is no sign-in, so scores do not follow you to a new phone.

I WANT MY LEADERBOARD ENTRY REMOVED

Email us the name exactly as it appears on the board and we will delete it. Removal is manual, so allow a few days.

THE GAME IS TOO HARD

Start with Levels rather than the Moon run — level 1 lasts about fifteen seconds and they build up gradually. Spending your bag in the refit shop on a spare hull or a bigger tank makes a large difference.

I SEE NO ADS, OR AN EMPTY STRIP

That is normal and not a fault. When no advert is available the space is given back to the game rather than left blank.

AM I SPENDING REAL MONEY?

No. There are no in-app purchases and no real currency anywhere in the game. The coins are simulated, have zero value, and cannot be bought, sold or cashed out. Nothing in the game is financial advice.
